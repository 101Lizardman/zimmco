How To Use This Prop

Somewhere nearby, there is always another machine.

ZimmCo Incorporated built durable transport, habitation domes and weapons for the outer-rim colonies — until its founders vanished and every factory went dark. One product outlived the company: the ZimmCo Refreshment Vending Machine. Millions were built, none are restocked, and almost none have failed. They stay unnaturally cold even long after they've lost power.

Press Find New Unit to roll up a machine — its Durability, stock, and flavour range. Vend dispenses a random can from what it carries, or tap a button on the pad to pull a specific flavour. Use the chips to track whether the unit gets cracked open, hacked, or bolts into Lock Down.

Beverages

Drinks are 25C each. All effects cease an hour after drinking if not utilized — and due to the nature of ZimmCo beverages, a human can only receive one effect's benefit every 6 hours.

Rumors

The first time your crew encounters a ZimmCo machine, roll a d10 for a rumor circulating about the company and its unmaintained hardware.
